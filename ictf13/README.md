# iCTF 2013 — Nuke 'em (or duck and cover!)

**Date:** Friday, December 6, 2013, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM PST
**Location:** Harold Frank Hall, UC Santa Barbara (Ground Floor, GSL/CISL)
**Format:** Attack-Defense (Nuclear Cyber-Warfare theme)
**Teams:** 100+ from universities worldwide (~1,200 students)
**Sponsor:** Lastline, Inc. — $1,024 cash prize for the winner

---

## The Story

In the 2013 iCTF, teams had to **develop their own nuclear weapon program**
while sustaining cyber-attacks whose goal was to block, delay, and hinder
the creation of a nuke. *(No real nukes were harmed in the making of this
competition.)*

This was **"Nuclear Cyber-Warfare"** in digital form: rival teams attacked
each other's nuclear development services while defending their own. The
competition, with over 100 teams and 1,200 students, was described at the
time as **"the world's largest hacking competition."**

The 12th edition of the iCTF, it marked a significant scaling milestone
and drew international media attention.

## Format

Classic attack-defense: each team was given an Internet server running
multiple services. Teams exploited vulnerabilities in rival teams' services
to capture flags while patching their own to stay defended.

- An automated scoring system tracked service availability and flag captures.
- The competition ran for 9 hours at UCSB's Harold Frank Hall.
