Subject: Media Advisory "Keeping Hackers at Bay" - Network Security - a 
    National First at UCSB



A national contest including multiple teams from UCSB College of 
Engineering, Georgia Tech, Naval Postgraduate School, NC State University, 
The United States Military Academy, and The University of Texas (Austin) 
will compete tomorrow (12/05/03 ; 1 - 5pm) in a first ever "live electronic 
intrusion" contest.

The event will be based in the Engineering I Building, adjacent to the 
GSL/CISL and hosted by Dr. Giovanni Vigna , Computer Science, College of 
Engineering.

(for further information please contact Dr. Vigna at: 893-7565, 637-6382)

This unique national exercise involves teams at different universities, 
spread across the nation competing simultaneously. This is the first time 
that such a collaboration has developed for a network security challenge at 
US Colleges.
