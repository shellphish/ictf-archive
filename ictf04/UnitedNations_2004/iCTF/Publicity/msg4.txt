Subject: Inter-university Capture the Flag Hacking Exercise

Folks,

	You may be interested in knowing that the Department of Computer Science
at UCSB is organizing a very unique event. A "capture the flag" hacking contest
that involves students from a number of Universities and institutions. This
exercise is organized as part of a graduate class on Network Security and
Intrusion Detection taught at UCSB.

The exercise will be held on December 5th and will involve: UCSB, GeorgiaTech,
Naval Postgraduate School, North Carolina State University, United States
Military Academy, University of Texas at Austin, University of Illinois at
Urbana-Champaign.

Previous editions of the class included "local" hacking contests as a form of
evaluation of the techniques learned in class. (Note that there are almost no
educational institutions in the US that offer anything like that)

This year we decide to extend the exercise to a number of universities and
institutions that are interested in security education. This is a very first
example of this type of exercise on such a large scale. You can find more
information at http://www.cs.ucsb.edu/~vigna/CTF

If you are interesting in covering it, let me know. I think that the students
would love that.
