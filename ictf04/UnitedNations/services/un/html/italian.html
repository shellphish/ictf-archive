Matteo Stallone

Matteo Stallone (brother of the more famous Sylvester) was born in Carugate, Italy on March 17, 1971. His program is centered on fostering partying throughout the world. He often declared that he doesn't care much about the United Nation and that he ended up being a candidate as the result of a prank. Nonetheless, he received enthusiatic support especially from the youngest portion of the world population.
