Talk of the United Nations

The rumors are true! We are changing the Charter of the United Nations!

You can contribute to this tremendous effort by connecting to our talkserver web-based discussion forum.

The application can be accessed with a normal browser by specifying the 8081 port. Once connected, you will have the opportunity to add annotations to the Charter of the UN. You will also able to review your annotations.

After January 1, 2015, the annotations provided by the UN citizens will be examined and discussed one by one in a plenary session. The most sensible annotations will be then inserted in the new Charter, which will be published on January 2, 2015.

Make the difference! Provide your comments on the new Charter!
