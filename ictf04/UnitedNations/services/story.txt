It's the 3rd of December 2014, and after endless debates it's finally the time
to vote the World President. The peoples of the world have their chance to
determine who is going to rule the World for the next four years.

The whole atmosphere is tense because the days before the elections have been
characterized by unconfirmed rumors that the Internet-based voting system of
these elections is not as safe as everybody thought.

The voting system (and the UN web site as well) are administered by the
Die-Bald corporation, a company from the nation of Leettonia, famous for
hosting the worst programmers in the world.

To make things worse, Massimo Zanardi, the shady administrator (and also CEO,
CTO, CFO, CSO, and CBO) of the Die-Bald corporation has suddenly disappeared,
leaving the election process in a mess.

A number of teams stepped up to pick up the pieces of the UN web site, creating
even more confusion. A hacker war among the teams started to demonstrate who
has the best security skills.
 
Your task is to keep the site up and running so that the election process can
smoothly proceed. At the same time, you will have to find out if the rumors
about the thousands of vulnerabilities are true and fix them if needed.  Of
course, in the meanwhile you have to demonstrate that your team is really l337
by showing how the security of the other teams' site can be compromised.

May the farce be with you!
