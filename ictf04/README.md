# iCTF 2004 — The 2004 United Nations Vote

**Date:** December 2004 (also a November 2004 preliminary round)
**Format:** Attack-Defense
**Teams:** 6+ international university teams

---

## The Story

The theme of the 2004 iCTF was a **futuristic world-wide voting event sponsored
by the United Nations**. Teams competed in an attack-defense format centered
on services representing a global online election system — voting infrastructure,
discussion boards, and authentication services for a fictional U.N. General Assembly
vote.

The competition had **serious performance problems**, resulting in a
partially-successful game. Because of this, a rematch was organized for
June 2005 — see [ictf04-05](../ictf04-05/).

## Participating Teams

Teams came from:
- UC Santa Barbara (ucsb)
- North Carolina State University (ncsu)
- Politecnico di Milano (Polimi)
- Polytechnic University Brooklyn (BrooklynPoly)
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (uiuc)
- West Point (usma)
