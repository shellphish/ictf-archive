# iCTF 2023 — AI and Cybersecurity

**Date:** December 2–8, 2023
**Format:** Jeopardy (two parallel tracks)
**Tracks:** High School + Undergraduate
**Organizers:** WiCS (Women in Computer Science) + Shellphish
**Sponsor:** NSF AI ACTION Institute

---

## The Story

The 2023 iCTF asked one question: **what happens when AI and hacking collide?**

As large language models began reshaping the security landscape, the iCTF
brought the future into the competition. For the first time, a new **LLM**
challenge category appeared alongside traditional pwn, crypto, and reversing.
Participants jailbroke chatbots, escaped AI-generated sandboxes, bypassed
content filters, and manipulated machine learning models.

The theme reflected a broader reality: AI systems are both powerful new
attack surfaces and novel tools for defenders. The iCTF framed this tension
as a series of puzzles — from injecting prompts to escape an "AI Calculator"
to reverse-engineering a neural network's hidden behavior.

### High School Track (22 challenges)

Includes easier variants of many undergraduate challenges plus exclusive
puzzles: `parakeet`, `parakeet_v2`, `rock paper scissors`, `Trendy`,
`Lost in Translation`, `Evil Model`, `Tensormania`, `Stop the spammer!`,
`guesstimate`, `Who is Waldo?`, plus shared challenges like AI Calculator,
AI Cryptic Game, CI Ninja, bytes_in_pairs, BrokenWords, and Mystical Castle.
