Announcement

Welcome to the 2014-2015 iCTF!

We had to reschedule a few times, but it's finally here!

At this point, you should have in your possession the virtual machine with the vulnerable services. Decrypt it, unpack it, start it, and enjoy the competition.

You will notice an unusually high number of services: this is because the theme of this competition is "Large-Scale Hacking: A Blast from the Past". So, can you hack 42 (the Answer to The Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything) services in 8 hours?

As you already know from the rules published on the web site a SUBSTANTIAL amount of points are given to teams who compromise the most services. Therefore, dive in, triage, assign, optimize, automate, do whatever you need to break into the most services.

Being leet is still important, but being able to hack FAST is also important. Once again, getting an exploit working before any other team will give you a serious advantage. Note that you cannot exploit another team's service directly: You need to create an exploit that we will run on your behalf. In order to be successful, you need to carefully follow the instructions.

Finally, remember that gaining points is useless if your services are down and your SLA is very low. Make sure you monitor closely your services and that they are up and functional. If a patch disrupts the normal operation of a service, the service will be marked as down, so patch carefully! And the SLA is averaged throughout the whole competition, so any interruption will have an effect on your final score.

Now go ahead, and hack 'em all!
