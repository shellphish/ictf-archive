The 2003 iCTF

The 2003 iCTF was the first CTF that involved other institutions other than UCSB.

Fourteen teams from around the United States competed in the game. The contest included teams from the following schools (some schools played as more than one team):

  • UCSB
  • North Carolina State University
  • Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey
  • West Point
  • Georgia Tech
  • University of Texas at Austin
  • University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign

The theme of the competition was the (fake) announcement that Microsoft Windows was going open-source.
