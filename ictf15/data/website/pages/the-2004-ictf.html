The 2004 iCTF

The theme of the 2004 iCTF, which was run in December 2004, was a futuristic world-wide voting event, sponsored by the United Nations.

The competition had serious performance problems, which resulted in a partially-successful game. Therefore, it was decided to organize a rematch in June 2005.
