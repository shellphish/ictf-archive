Mission

Our mission was to detect intrusion on coppola and hide the flag. To this end, we had several responsibilities. First, we had to protect secret.txt mounted on host coppola. To do this, we decided to put the real secret.txt very deep in directory hierarchy (~3000 levels) and put a multiple copies of fake secret.txt all over the place. We had to monitor the activities on the host itself and detecting any unauthorized access / malicious activity. For this, we monitored lots of logs, used IP chains, TCP wrappers, Tripwire, and some UNIX commands like lsof, who, netstat, strace, etc.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Sunita/Madhavan: Learning, configuring rules for IP chains and TCP wrappers and monitoring/maintaining respective logs during the exercise.
  • Amit/Vishal: Writing scripts to embed secret.txt deep, putting other fake secret.txt's, installing and configuring Snort.
  • Doli: Installing and configuring Tripwire, and monitoring activities during the war using it, helping out with Snort.
