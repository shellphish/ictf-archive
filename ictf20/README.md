# iCTF 2020 — Abbey Road (1 After 909)

**Date:** December 2020 (exact date not documented)
**Format:** Attack-Defense
**Theme:** The Beatles

---

## The Story

The 2020 iCTF was a love letter to The Beatles. Every service was named
after a famous song from the band's catalog — from the psychedelic trip
of *Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds* to the submarine voyage of
*Yellow Submarine*.

The competition title, **"1 After 909"**, is itself a Beatles song,
recorded at the famous Get Back sessions and appearing on the *Let It Be*
album. It's the song that closes Side One — a nod to the year 2020 being
"one after" the year everything changed.
