Xmorph - Image morphing program

Xmorph and tkmorph are digital image warping programs: they read, write, warp, and dissolve images, and read, write, create, and manipulate control meshes wich determine the warping.

Xmorph runs under the X Window System, and can be run as a GIMP plug-in.
