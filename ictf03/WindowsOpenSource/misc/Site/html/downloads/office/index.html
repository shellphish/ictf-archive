Conference Wrap-Ups
In the last month alone, OpenOffice.org members have, at considerable personal expense and labor, organized and staffed exhibition booths at several key trade shows and conferences throughout the world. Members have also presented papers on OpenOffice.org's technology and on the project's general importance to open-source development and acceptance by governments, businesses, and individuals everywhere. Our presence at most conferences is sought after: OpenOffice.org represents not just the future of the desktop office suite, but of open-source. [More...]


Interview: Joerg Heilig, Director, Software Engineering, Sun

Joerg Heilig has perhaps contributed more than any other person to the worldwide success of OpenOffice.org and StarOffice--and has done so quietly. Located in Hamburg, Germany, Joerg oversees the technical development of the OpenOffice.org/StarOffice code. This interview, conducted via email earlier this week, provides some insight into the work being done.
