# iCTF 2003 — Windows Goes Open-Source

**Date:** November–December 2003 (two rounds: Nov 14 and Dec 5)
**Format:** Attack-Defense
**Teams:** 14 (from 7 US universities)

---

## The Story

The 2003 iCTF was the **first edition to involve institutions other than
UC Santa Barbara**. Fourteen teams from around the United States competed.

The theme was the (fictitious) announcement that **Microsoft Windows was going
open-source**. Teams had to attack and defend services that played out this
fictional corporate/technology scenario.

## Participating Universities

- UC Santa Barbara
- North Carolina State University
- Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey
- West Point (United States Military Academy)
- Georgia Tech
- University of Texas at Austin
- University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign

(Some schools fielded more than one team, totaling 14 teams.)

## Format

Classic attack-defense: each team defended their own set of services while
attacking other teams' identical copies.
