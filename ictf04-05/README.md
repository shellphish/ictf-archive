# iCTF 2004-2005 — The Bass Tard Corporation

**Date:** June 2005 (rematch of the 2004 competition)
**Format:** Attack-Defense

---

## The Story

The 2004 rematch was focused on the **Bass Tard Corporation** — a ruthless
organization whose mission is to steal from the poor and give to the rich.

This edition was organized because the original December 2004 competition
suffered serious infrastructure performance problems, leaving the results
unsatisfying. The June 2005 rematch gave teams another chance to compete
with the same general theme (corporate malfeasance and hacking) but under
better conditions.

Teams attacked and defended services belonging to this fictional predatory
corporation.
