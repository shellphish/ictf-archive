# iCTF 2024

**Date:** November 16–22, 2024
**Format:** Jeopardy (two parallel tracks + training)
**Tracks:** High School + Undergraduate + Training

---

## Overview

The 2024 iCTF continued the dual-track structure introduced in ictf23,
offering separate High School and Undergraduate competitions running
simultaneously on CTFd. A training track — reusing a selection of ictf23
challenges — helped newcomers warm up before the main event.

The challenge mix expanded the **machine-learning** category (adversarial
AI, model extraction, and backdoor analysis) while returning to classic
categories: web exploitation, binary pwn, forensics, and programming.

## High School Track (31 challenges)

Includes most undergraduate challenges at lower difficulty, plus High School
exclusives: `movie_time`, `hidden_number`, `gpt_calculator`, `Babychecker`,
`Chromapyide`, `the_stand` (knowledge category), and `mayor_setter(again)`.

## Training Challenges (19 challenges)

A curated subset of ictf23 challenges for practice: includes BrokenWords,
parakeet/parakeet_v2, Lost in Translation, AI Calculator, AI Cryptic Game,
rock paper scissors, Evil Model, Tensormania, Stop the spammer!, guesstimate,
escape_from_markov, token_of_appreciation, CI Ninja, bytes_in_pairs,
aimazing, and Mystical Castle.
