# iCTF 2007 — The Copyright Mafia

**Date:** December 2007
**Format:** Attack-Defense

---

## The Story

The 2007 iCTF's theme was **"The Copyright Mafia"** — a group that was a mix
between organized crime and big corporation-type copyright enforcement practices.

Teams played as rival factions within this criminal-corporate hybrid,
attacking and defending the Mafia's extortion, enforcement, and intimidation
infrastructure. The competition had explicit game-theory elements: the
`mafia2007Scripts/` archive includes bot scripts for **petition** (pleading
for mercy), **amends** (making reparations), and **shakedown** (extorting
rivals) — suggesting that teams could take these actions against each other
as part of the competition mechanics, not just exploit services.
