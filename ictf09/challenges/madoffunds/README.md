# iCTF 2009 — MadoFunds

**Category:** Misc
**Tags:** web, economy

## Description

MadoFunds (named after Bernie Madoff's infamous Ponzi scheme, which was
front-page news in December 2009) was the in-game currency exchange. Teams
earned "leetness points" by solving standalone CTF challenges, then converted
them into in-game money at MadoFunds. The exchange rate fluctuated during the
competition. Money was used in the overall team ranking.
