History

The Bass Tard corporation was funded in 1889 by Mr. B. Tard, a disgustingly rich millionaire whose favorite activity was to steal the lunch boxes of elementary school kids who belonged to family with financial problems.

Mr. Tard decided to fund the Bass Tard Corporation so that he could both evade taxes and continue his crusade against poverty or, more precisely, against the poor.

In more than a hundred years, the company evolved from a mere 500 employees to the current 10,000 employees that devote their energy to make the rich even richer and the poor even poorer world-wide.
