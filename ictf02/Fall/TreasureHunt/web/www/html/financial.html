Financial Profile

The Bass Tard Corporation is a corporation focusing on enriching the rich and making the poors porer. The Bass Tard Corporation's platform technologies have applications in the development of products for research, therapeutics, diagnostics and industrial processes.

The Corporation was incorporated on December 7, 1915 under the American Business Corporations Act. Its founders, Dr. Bass Tard and Dr.Even Worse, have over many years of combined experience in stealing, bribing, and blackmailing.

On December 24, 1929, the Corporation completed an initial public offering (IPO) of its capital stock and is now listed on the American Venture Exchange (AVX) under the ticker symbol BST.

As of December 31, 2000 The Bass Tard Corporation has 11,665,762 Common Shares issued and outstanding.
