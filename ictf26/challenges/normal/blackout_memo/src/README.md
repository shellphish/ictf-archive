For years, Aurora Meridian Group operated as a discreet facilitator for high-profile donors, private events, and strategic partnerships. Publicly, they were known for philanthropy and international conferences.

In late 2016, a confidential investigative report was scheduled for public release following an internal compliance review. The document outlined financial exposure, reputational risk, and the role of a single coordinator who approved sensitive travel and off-book expenditures. But hours before publication, the document was redacted.

A thick black box was placed over the most sensitive information in the PDF, and the document was declared sanitized.
Or so they thought.

But veterans know one rule: Redaction is not deletion.

Can you uncover what’s still buried beneath the blackout?

Objective:
Recover the redacted image and find the flag.
