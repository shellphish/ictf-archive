Discuss It!

As the World-Wide Elections approach, people may feel lost and unable to make such a hard decision... "Who should I vote for?", "Does my vote count?", "Who is the totally cute candidate?"

These and other important question have been asked to the UN offices for the past few months. To help people deal with the difficult choices to be made in this elections we are offering an online discussion service, called "Discuss-It".

By telnetting to port 7413 you will be able to contact a human operator that will be happy to discuss with you the important issues of these elections.

Please feel free to spend hours talking to informed, impartial expert about what you feel and the decisions you are about to make. The conversation is confidential and the security of the service is granted by the high-quality products provided by the Die-Bald Corporation.

Enjoy!
